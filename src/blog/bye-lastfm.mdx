It has been nice scrobbling to last.fm almost every day for the past two and a half years, but now, over 48000 scrobbles later I depart from this platform.

I wanted to move off of this zombie shell of a platform for a few months and I've finally been pushed by
[this](https://support.last.fm/t/api-keys-will-require-pro-from-1st-december/121030) announcement by last.fm staff. TLDR for anyone not in the loop: api access
will require a pro subscription. This was announced only 12 days before the change goes into effect, too short of a notice in my opinion.

Listenbrainz is a great alternative, but I've opted for completely owning my data by self-hosting [multi-scrobbler](https://docs.multi-scrobbler.app/). Setup was
quick and easy. Works with a buch of apps including spotify so I'm more than happy with it. I'll see how stable its going to be on my shitty old laptop ""server""...
Though so far it has been great.

As for showing off my scrobbles, I set up a [koito](https://koito.io/) instance (check it out [here](https://koito.kabeyai.net/)).

I understand that the restriction was made to combat scrapers, though a longer heads up (at least three months) or a free tier with a more unforgiving rate limit
could have been tested first before being fully paywalled.

## "Just get the subscription"

As of writing this, it's $5 a month, for an American that isn't a lot, but for people from other countries it can be way more. See this table of countries my online friends
are from:

_I tried to find median wage where I could, but most of these are the average wage per hour. Whereever the hourly wage wasn't listed, it was calculated
for a 40h work week. Keep in mind most people typically earn less than the published numbers suggest._

| Country     | Hourly wage             | Time to earn $5 |
| ----------- | ----------------------- | --------------- |
| USA         | $24.51[^1]              | 12.2 min        |
| Germany     | $23.49 (€21.48)[^2]     | 12.8 min        |
| Czechia     | $12.41 (277.11 Kč)[^3]  | 24.2 min        |
| Brazil      | $4.29 (R$ 23.83)[^4]    | 1.2 h           |
| Phillipines | $2.11 (₱134.65)[^5]     | 2.4 h           |
| Indonesia   | $0.94 (Rp17 188.35)[^6] | 5.3 h           |

Five dollars simply isn't as little in other countries. And last.fm does not implement regional pricing. You cannot share an api key issued to you with others and
operating a proxy for others that injects your own key server-side is legally dubious.
