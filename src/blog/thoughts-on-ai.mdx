This is specifically about **generative AI**, not other forms of _genuinely helpful_ forms of AI, such as deep learning, translation or voice recognition.

It is a tool. It is **not a replacement** for human creativity.

It can be utilized to automate mundane tasks to allow humans to focus on what they truly enjoy.
I for example used Claude to write me a shell script which sorted all my screenshots (3880 files as of writing this!) and normalized their filenames,
as they used about 6 different naming conventions. This saved me a big headache trying to write my own script. Though I had to test it and
catch bugs, it was still faster than me figuring out shell scripting and I had my screenshots sorted within 30 minutes.

This might come across as strange, considering I am a member of the [no ai webring](https://baccyflap.com/noai/).
It is up to everyone to draw their line in the sand.

I think that vibecoding is a problem and I'll always prefer hand-coded programs over vibecoded slop. I personally would not be comfortable
pushing vibecoded code into production. But if people want to waste megawatts of power to make another shitty app that no one will use, sure.
The person who you're shooting in the foot is yourself. Gen-AI is able to generate larger-scale projects, but as soon as something goes
horribly wrong, you're screwed. Unless, of course, you have enough money to burn.

With that being said, I need to rewrite my site's backend since a big portion of the code is AI
generated (because I was too lazy to learn expressjs and sqlite libraries). Progress is there, I'm using Fastify now
and I'm hoping to have that new version deployed within a month or two.

Personally, I will never generate content. Be it blogposts, social media posts, images, music, website styles, etc...
Regarding code though, I personally adhere to the [not by AI 90%](https://notbyai.fyi/not-by-ai-90-rule) rule.

I use gen AI to aid in learning new frameworks, libraries and debugging errors. It is way faster to point an agent to an error and have it explain why
that happens and then implement the fix myself rather than googling for 30 minutes and then bashing my head against the wall for another 30.
