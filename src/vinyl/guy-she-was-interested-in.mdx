For those wondering why this contains such a random track list, I believe it's music that the manga characters listen to.

Unfortunately I had a _little_ accident and scratched the A side across nearly the whole radius. It's not deep but you can hear a crack every so often when you listen.
Unsure whether that warrants shelling out 60 euro for a new record (though knowing me I might actually do that).
