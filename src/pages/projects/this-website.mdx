Now I have **multiple** pages: [one](/collections/) with buttons and stamps, [other](/blog/) with my blog and [another](/vinyl/) with my vinyl collection and some more.

I've also made some great friends over my (relatively short) time on the inide web. If you haven't seen their websites yet, I can definitely recommend you to check out
[ivrry.nekoweb.org](https://ivrry.nekoweb.org/) by Noelle and [jbc.lol](https://jbc.lol/) by <span title="Jonathan Butter">JB</span>!

If you're thinking about making your own website, give it a shot! It's quite fun! Browse [nekoweb](https://nekoweb.org/) and [neocities](https://neocities.org/) for inspiration
or just to have fun exploring. And when you find something cool, use the "Inspect Element" feature in your browser to see how they did that and learn. That's how I started
out anyway... AI can be helpful when learning if you ask the right questions and stay curious, but if you let it generate your site for you instead of you actually
making it yourself you're missing the point of a personal site.

Oh an if you're interested in looking at the source code, it is freely available [here](https://github.com/wirelessfish/website)!
