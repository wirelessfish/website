I can recommend this vinyl to anyone who loves Nirvana and is considering getting one. It's not the popular Nevermind album, sure, but it is widely regarded as one
of the best MTV Unplugged albums out there (along with Alice in Chains unplugged).

Again as with [Wasting Light](/vinyl/wasting-light), the vinyl sound beats spotify losless in the sound range. Especially with speakers. It makes you feel as if
you were really there. My only gripe is that they didn't include all the small-talk between individual tracks. It would probably require a 3rd record though,
so I understand why they didn't. The full version can be found on [youtube](https://youtu.be/tluCDea1YQw). I suggest you take a listen,
it's quite funny at times!
