This has to be one of my favourite albums from Foo Fighters. It's up there with But Here We Are and Foo Fighters (the album, lol).
Honestly if I were to list all my favourite tracks I'd list half the album, so I'll just tell you which
one is my #1 favourie: it's tied between Back & Forth and Bridge Burning.

I cannot recommend it enough. If you've haven't listened to it yet, you're missing out!

I can also confidently say that the difference between spotify's losless stream and the vinyl was larger than I initially expected (coming from someone who
never heard a vinyl before). It's marginal with headphones, you notice it more with actual speakers.
But the vinyl definitely feels more dynamic, as if the audio range of the spotify stream was flat/cut off at the top and bottom.

### Gallery

The album itself comes packaged in a beautiful gatefold and both of the 2 records also get their own sleeve. I like how the 4 sides of these records have the corresponding
CMYK colors which the art works with beautifully.
